📖 Complete Guide & Documentation

Master the Linkstream platform with our comprehensive step-by-step documentation.

1

Copy Your Media URL

Navigate to any supported video or audio platform. Click the "Share" button and choose "Copy Link" or copy the URL directly from your browser's address bar. Make sure the link is publicly accessible.

Supported: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=... | https://instagram.com/reel/...
2

Paste & Analyze Formats

Paste the copied link into the Linkstream extraction card at the top of this page. Click ⚡ Extract Media. Our cloud engine queries available stream manifests and returns every quality option sorted from highest resolution (4K/1080p) to audio-only.

3

Automated Stream Merging & Download

Select your desired resolution or bitrate pill. When you click Download Selected Quality, our FastAPI + FFmpeg backend processes the stream, merges high-definition video with crystal-clear audio, and sends the finalized file directly to your device.

Developer API Endpoints

Linkstream provides clean JSON endpoints for developers looking to integrate media extraction into their own tools:
